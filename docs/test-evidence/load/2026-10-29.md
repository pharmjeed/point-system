## Observations
- **A pre-run with the same profile** (run `af4efec4`, commit `ecfa053`, 04:55 UTC) measured M1 p95 155 ms, M2 p95 72 ms,
  0 errors in 2000 requests and 100/100 delivered 72.9 s after the first press. Its only failed check was the end-of-run
  outbox check, which then counted one `job.offer_expire` that had fallen due in the second the check ran (a no-op timer
  of an offer accepted long before, taken by the worker's next 500 ms poll). The check now uses the system monitor's
  own rule — due longer than `ops.outbox_lag_alert_s` (30 s), a lapsed lease, failed or dead — and reports the rows due
  at that instant beside it (commit `8a5e224`); the evidence run above passed every check.
- **No contention at this load.** With 10 drivers per venue and 20 requests per venue in the minute, every request
  went out as a single offer to the longest-idle available driver and was accepted on its first round
  (`point_dispatch_offers_total{mode="single",outcome="accepted"} 100`, no broadcast, no escalation, no 409/410). The
  average time to claim, 5.8 s, is the virtual drivers' 1–10 s decision time. Parallel accepts, the priority guard and
  next-task reservations are proven by the race suite (`pnpm test:race`, PNT-063), not by this profile.
- **Where the time goes.** A press is acknowledged in 5 ms (p50; the webhook only stores and enqueues), the worker turns
  it into a cycle and an offer within ~10–100 ms, and the realtime publication reaches the 9 other drivers of the pool
  in 38 ms (p50) after the accept's transaction began. No database lock wait was recorded
  (`point_db_lock_wait_seconds_count 0`).
- **Database connections.** The api opened 15 connections at the burst (20 in the pre-run, its `DATABASE_POOL_MAX`)
  without lock waits or latency impact; the worker used 10. A bigger deployment sizes `DATABASE_POOL_MAX` per process
  against the server's `max_connections` (05 §7).
- **Representativeness.** The machine is a 4-vCPU development host shared with other build jobs (17 §11 asks for
  staging sized like production). Repeat the run there before the pilot and before any expansion (17 §11, Q-08):
  `pnpm ops:load --rate <n>`; the harness creates its own `point_load_*` database and never touches another.
